RescueBite FOOD RESCUE — DONOR CLAIMS + RESPONSIVE LAYOUT UPDATE

This archive includes the quantity-based recipient flow plus all three donor features:
1. Claims received dashboard for the logged-in donor's own food listings.
2. Recipient details including name/email, reserved quantity, reservation time, pickup location and deadline.
3. Reservation confirmation, pickup tracking and completion.

It also preserves the My Claims page, recipient cancellation and stock restoration, and adds donor cancellation beside the active workflow actions.

LAYOUT IMPROVEMENTS
- Adds spacing between the Claims received heading/subtitle and claim cards.
- Aligns workflow action buttons at the bottom of donor claim cards.
- Places Confirm reservation + Cancel and Mark picked up + Cancel side by side.
- Makes the Claims received navigation and card grid more usable in split-screen and narrow windows.
- Cancellation is available while a claim is reserved or confirmed. Once picked up, cancellation is disabled because the handover has begun.

BEFORE REPLACING FILES
- Stop the frontend and backend terminals with Ctrl+C.
- Back up your existing project folder.
- Keep your existing server/.env and client/.env files; they are intentionally not included.
- Replace the matching files while preserving folder structure. At minimum, replace:
  client/src/App.jsx
  client/src/styles.css
  server/src/routes/foodRoutes.js
- Also copy server/src/models/Claim.js and server/src/models/Food.js from this archive if they are not already the quantity-based versions included in your working project.
